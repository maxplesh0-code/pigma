our technology

your data is lying to you.

Not because the numbers are wrong. Because they are only telling you half the story. Knowing something went wrong is one thing. Having the intelligence to understand it, and improve it, is another.

Your latest report might tell you deliveries were late, freight costs increased or orders took longer to despatch. But does it tell you why? Was it a picking delay? An inefficient route? A carrier issue? A breakdown between systems?

Our technology brings warehouse, freight and transport information together, giving you one connected picture instead of five different systems. From real-time inventory and order status through to freight tracking, PODs and operational reporting, the information is there when it is needed.

see everything. miss nothing.

Real-time consignment tracking across every carrier from a single dashboard. Status updates, location data and predicted ETA in one view, with event-level detail on every scan, pickup and delivery attempt.

  • Multi-carrier tracking consolidated into one screen
  • Live GPS driver and order tracking with mapping
  • Automated status polling and full event logging
  • Customer notification triggers by SMS and email

business intelligence that actually means business.

There is a difference between having data and knowing what to do with it. Our specialists use our business intelligence and reporting to assess performance, identify inefficiencies and recommend improvements.

  • DIFOT: are deliveries arriving in full and on time?
  • Order processing: where are picking and despatch delays occurring?
  • Transit performance: which routes or services need attention?
  • Freight costs: where are the opportunities to improve efficiency?

industries

same platform. different playbook.

elite services three primary industries: retail, FMCG and light industrial. The warehouse, the fleet and the carrier network stay the same. What changes is how we run them and what we report on.

retail

Fashion retail is the largest industry we provide solutions for and the field we built our business around. We provide 3PL warehousing and retail transport to all major shopping complexes in metropolitan Melbourne, and scale nationally for rollouts.

  • Store-to-store, DC-to-store and store-to-DFO delivery
  • RF scanning and real-time remote inventory monitoring
  • Barcoding, tagging, garment hanging and pre-shipment services
  • Seasonal injections and new store rollouts, reported daily

fmcg

Speed is the key factor in fast-moving consumer goods. Optimised pick-pack, algorithm-driven picking paths and 24 hour order processing, with the reporting to prove the turnaround.

  • 24 hour order processing on e-commerce brands
  • Real-time tracking from pick through to proof of delivery
  • High velocity despatch for homewares and electrical
  • Scalability through promotional and peak periods

light industries

The light industrial sector covers construction, mining and energy. Heavier, lumpier, less predictable freight, handled with scalable storage, contract warehousing and freight management in a single environment.

  • Bulk storage and contract warehousing that scales
  • Cloud-based inventory management and data analysis
  • Freight management across multiple carriers and lanes
  • Industry specialists reviewing cost and performance
