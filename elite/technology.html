WHY IT MATTERS

Your data is lying to you.

Not because the numbers are wrong. Because they are only telling you half the story. Your report might say deliveries were late, freight costs rose, or orders took longer to despatch. Does it tell you why?

Was it a picking delay? An inefficient route? A carrier issue? A breakdown between systems? Knowing something went wrong is one thing. Having the intelligence to understand it, and improve it, is another.
